01 / A little about me

Curious by nature.
Builder by habit.

My sweet spot sits where data science meets software engineering: make the analysis useful, make the system repeatable, and make the result easy to trust.

I’m pursuing a B.Tech in Data Science at NMIMS MPSTME, Mumbai (2024–2028). I enjoy working end to end — from cleaning and querying data to training models, building APIs, and thinking through how a system behaves once it is running.

My recent work includes a reproducible demand-forecasting pipeline with model tracking and monitoring, plus SQL projects focused on exploring real-world datasets and improving data quality. I’m also interested in RAG, agentic workflows, and reliable ML systems.

I value clear reasoning over black boxes, measurable checks over assumptions, and projects that solve a real problem rather than stopping at a demo.
